My Interests: Bodybuilding

Bodybuilding

I love bodybuilding because it has helped me to feel comfortable in my skin. I used to feel too skinny compared to other kids when I was younger and it affected my self-esteem, because I felt small and weak. My older brother inspired me to begin bodybuilding through his constant training in the gym, especially when I witnessed him compete. It made me realize that I'm capable of doing the same thing. So I put in work from high school and a quickly became addicted to training and seeing progress before my eyes.

I believe that taking care of your body not only increases your longevity, but it also does something to your confidence. Alongside that it can increase your strength which can be carried to most everyday tasks like lifting heavy objects and playing sports.
