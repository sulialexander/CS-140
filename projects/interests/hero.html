Hero: Arnold Schwarzenegger

About Arnold Schwarzenegger:

Arnold Schwarzenegger is famously known as one of the greatest bodybuilders of all time. This is not only due to his success but also the things he did to get where he is today. He was from a small town in Austria and people in his town were content and often spent most of their life in that town. But Arnold had a dream combined with determination and resilience which led him to win consecutive Mr. Olympia competitions, becoming a Hollywood actor and eventually the governor of California. He teaches all people that with hard work and willingness any vision can be brought to fruition.

Arnold's Achievements:

Olympia
Arnold won the Mr. Olympia competition 6 times in a row from 1970 to 1975 and then again in 1980.
Hollywood Actor
Arnold became a Hollywood actor and starred in several well known films such as the Terminator and Predator.
Governor of California
Arnold became the governor of California from 2003 to 2011.
