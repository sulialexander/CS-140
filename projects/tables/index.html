Tonga's Rugby League World Cup Journey

In 2017, Jason Taumalolo decided to play for Tonga over New Zealand, ultimately choosing heritage and blood over money. Other players also chose Tonga over their usual World Cup teams, and it paid off: they beat New Zealand, one of the best teams in the cup, and made it all the way to the semi-finals. Two years later, in 2019, they beat Australia too. This helped inspire Samoan players to do the same thing and choose the Samoa team over money.
