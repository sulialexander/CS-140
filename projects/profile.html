I served a mission in Samoa and one of my most valuable abilities I developed was recognising the Holy Spirits voice and acting on it. Anything that is good comes directly from the Saviour and likewise the opposite comes from the adversary which means that every good thought is the Spirit speaking to you. I applied this when I would carry out door knocking I would listen for which houses I should visit and messages I should share which is important because different people have different needs as to what they need to hear and timing is also an important factor.

A key moment on my mission was receiving a witness that The Book of Mormon is true. I read a chapter in Nephi that help me to understand why The Book of Mormon was neccesary and why we preach it more than the Bible. Having this realisation and receiving this witness moved me to share the message of The Book of Mormon with every person who was able to hear it.
