Why

Photography and filmmaking are my passions, and I want to bless people with my talent by capturing their special moments through photos or videos. The site enables people to gain a taste of my style and how my creativity can make their moments memorable. Couples in Hawaii who are getting engaged can book me to shoot their engagement live or capture post-engagement photos to create posts for invites and maybe just for posting on social media, or just to remember the time they were engaged before they got married. Graduates in my college can also visit and see my style too.
